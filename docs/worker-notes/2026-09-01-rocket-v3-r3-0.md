## Decisions and assumptions

The corrected taxonomy is authoritative for this task. `Batch_site` remains a structural member of the Core `origin_site` sum but is not an Origin-family identity. Its anonymous identity is the separate `Batch` family represented by `batch_id`. No synthetic Origin ID or `Batch_origin` family is introduced.

`ScopedRole` is the six-family name for the V2 format’s scope-qualified Role identity. Program and item-template roles share the scoped label space but remain distinct through scope qualification. `ProgramRoot`, `ProgramScope`, `ProgramComplete`, `EntryGuard` and branch `Stop` are structural concepts/terminals, not anonymous identity families.

The inventory treats Enc_V2 as the byte authority and Rocket V2 refinement as incomplete evidence. It does not infer canonical identity from raw IDs, storage order, V2 heuristic colour rank or equal refinement colour.
