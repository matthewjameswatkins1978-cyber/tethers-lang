# Replay Digest Shape
# Source: src/replay.rs, src/replay_windows.rs

Replay claims use SHA-256 digests over canonicalized JSON payloads.
Each claim is a generation in a ledger chain (generations 0-2, gen 3 is terminal).

Claim shape:
- generation: 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 (terminal)
- logical_key: (event_id, tether_id, action_index) tuple
- execution_identity: UUIDv4
- predecessor_digest: null (gen 0) | SHA-256 of previous claim
- claim_digest: SHA-256 of canonicalized JSON body (JCS RFC 8785)
- state: claim_only | armed | succeeded | failed | uncertain
- binding: replay-specific binding that constrains re-execution

The digest covers all fields; any mutation invalidates the chain.
